Channel Factory

A private tool that helps one creator manage their own YouTube channels.

What it does

Channel Factory uploads videos the creator has made, updates their titles and descriptions, and reads and replies to comments on the creator's own channels. It uses the YouTube Data API with the creator's permission.

Who can use it

Channel Factory is not a public service. It is used only by its owner to manage channels they own. There are no other users and no sign-up.
