Privacy Policy

Channel Factory · Effective November 11, 2026

Channel Factory is a private tool used by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

YouTube API Services

Channel Factory uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

  • Basic information about the owner's YouTube channels, such as channel names and IDs.
  • The owner's videos and their details (titles, descriptions, upload settings), in order to upload and update them.
  • Public comments on the owner's videos, in order to read them and post replies.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos, update their details, and read and reply to comments on the owner's channels. The text of comments may be processed by an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude), used by the owner, to help draft replies.

Storage and security

Access tokens and working files are stored only on the owner's own computer. Channel Factory has no servers and no database of its own.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except the AI assistant described above, used solely to draft replies for the owner.

Retention and deletion

Comment data is kept only as long as needed to draft and post replies. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings. Revoking access stops Channel Factory from reaching any YouTube data. To ask for any stored data to be deleted, contact the email below.
